# Agent Contract Standard

Every agent must make these sections discoverable in its definition:

- **Mission:** why the agent exists.
- **Expertise:** what it is qualified to do.
- **Responsibilities:** what it owns.
- **Boundaries:** what it must not decide or modify.
- **Inputs:** what it needs before acting.
- **Outputs:** what it must return.
- **Collaboration:** who it consults and when.
- **Handoff:** required handoff structure.
- **Quality gates:** how work is verified.
- **Escalation:** when it must stop and ask.

A role title alone is not a contract.
